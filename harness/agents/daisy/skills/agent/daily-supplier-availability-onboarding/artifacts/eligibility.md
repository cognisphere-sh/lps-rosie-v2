# Texas-first gate (read-only)

Decide and record each supplier's outcome before any email search, list
parsing, contact lookup, newsletter attempt or draft. LPS serves Texas only
for now (`knowledge/memory.md`).

| Outcome | When | Then |
|---|---|---|
| `retained-future-market` | Out-of-state `Wholesale Supplier` | Record category/state evidence. No research, signup, draft or contact this run. Keep it in state; don't retire or mark it in the database. |
| `eligible-Texas-service` | Clear evidence they serve Texas: delivery/service-area statement, sales terms, a direct business fact | Go to [research](research.md). Growers first, then Texas wholesale nurseries. |
| `held-service-to-Texas-unverified` | No or conflicting Texas-service evidence | Only the read-only research needed to record the gap; stop. |
| `held-service-to-Texas-unverified-prior-qualified` | Same, but the supplier is verified original-cohort | A focused read-only look; if still unclear, queue a question for a later, Chris-approved supplier-thread message: "Do you have an availability list, and do you ship to or have customers in Texas?" (Chris, 2026-08-28). No draft or send now. |
| Placeholder hold | Chris named the supplier a placeholder (e.g. Devil Mountain: big specimen trees only, 2026-08-20) | Record the hold, move on. |

Evidence rules:

- A Texas address, city, ZIP or the Texas market list is not proof of
  service.
- **Original cohort** (Chris's original sheet): verified only when this exact
  company is tied to a row of that list; record the label, file/URL and
  retrieval date. It is a prior-qualification signal, not proof of
  availability, price, Texas delivery or permission to contact.
- **Trade associations** (e.g. TNLA membership for La Croix Nursery; an
  Alabama association for a Louisiana supplier's reach): supporting evidence
  when current and clearly theirs (record name, URL/date, geography). Direct
  service statements win. Decisive only where Chris decided that case.
- Existing drafts are records: never send, delete, copy or revive one because
  of this gate. For held/retained suppliers record draft id, recipient,
  subject and `draft-held-market-gate`.
