# Supplier communications

Daisy writes to suppliers about onboarding, accounts, paperwork, records and
availability channels, from `chris.r@localplantsupply.com`. Rosie handles all
job and order contact from `chris@localplantsupply.com`. Decide by subject.

| Task | Read |
|---|---|
| Post an arriving email or any contact to the supplier panel | [transcript](artifacts/transcript.md) |
| Draft or send a supplier email | [send](artifacts/send.md) |
| Where a fact belongs; daily note; workspace index | [records](artifacts/records.md) |

- Never email a supplier unless Chris or Bhoumik asked for that contact, and
  approved the exact draft. A template, a missing price or a supplier's reply
  is not permission. Chris handles formal gated buyer accounts himself.
- Work on the supplier's own thread (`daisy_thread_id`). Other threads pass
  the exact request there.
- Mixed email (a list plus an order question): ingest the list; give Rosie
  the order part internally (identifiers, mailbox, exact text); don't send a
  second reply. She can't read Daisy's mailbox.
- Internal handoffs never go to suppliers or customers.
