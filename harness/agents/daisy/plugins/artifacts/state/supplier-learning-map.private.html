The big picture

What the labels mean

Grower

Production-led partner

Primary production at scale, generally serving a broader wholesale market. This is the first onboarding priority in Texas.

Wholesale nursery

Regional trade supply

Often a sales-yard or regional trade-supply model; may grow some material. Relevant in Texas as a secondary relationship prospect.

Retail nursery

Different operating model

High-touch operations and costs can make recurring buying lower volume. It should not be treated as the same lane as grower onboarding.

Broker / platform

Different role again

A broker or platform is a distinct market role—not a proxy for either a grower or a wholesale nursery.

Segmentation is not the app’s current type field

Original source-list cohorts were internal sourcing/outreach segments: Wholesale Growers (Texas / out of Texas), Wholesale Nurseries (Texas / out of Texas), Retail Nurseries (Texas), Platform & Brokers, and Luxury Builders.

Learning: preserve that strategic segmentation rather than collapsing it into the current generic type enumeration. The cohort labels express intended market work; they are not a simple identity claim about every supplier.

Relationship approach

1

Start with growers

Prioritize Texas growers for onboarding and availability work.

2

Build light-touch wholesale-nursery ties

Many use account applications and a primary contact gate. The goal is respectful relationship engagement—not an immediate demand for lists.

3

Keep future markets visible

Out-of-state wholesale nurseries remain useful future-market references, but receive no routine availability, contact, newsletter, or POC effort now.

Southwest is a high-potential benchmark: Chris completes its application; no outreach/contact action is implied by this learning map.
