# The LPS business

Lily's working picture of how Local Plant Supply actually runs — drawn from
Chris's feedback (`../feedback.md`), from the app and docs, and from what the
other agents hand over. **Living documents, revised in place** — unlike the
log, which is append-only. Record where a fact came from (a date, or a log
handle) so a stale belief can be traced and corrected.

Understanding the business means knowing both what Chris does and what the
software does today: `../../../../../app/`, `docs/app/`, `docs/harness/` and
`docs/supabase-schema.md` are the other half of the picture.

## Files

- `operations.md` — current LPS operations, including one-JR quick quoting/later sourcing, customer commitment versus supplier verification, and separate future dashboard/history scope.
- `friction.md` — recurring workflow gaps and the evidence for improving them.
