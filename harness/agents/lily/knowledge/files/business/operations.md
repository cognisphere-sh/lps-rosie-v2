## Supplier availability outreach

- **2026-08-28 — Non-Partner availability emails should read as a normal human ask (Chris, JR-21 test):** Suppliers do not understand internal LPS terminology, so an availability email must not describe its purpose as an “LPS job.” Chris wants a brief, light, informal request with concrete project/location context where known and appropriate, followed by the plant, size and quantity and a direct question about whether it is available. “Please confirm your availability” is too demanding; asking “Y’all have those available?” is the intended posture. A photo request can be included when it would help assess material, not as boilerplate. This applies to the Chris-approved, non-Partner outreach flow; recorded supplier-specific preferences still win. Source: feedback log, **Human supplier availability asks** (2026-08-28); Rosie supplier-coordination and job-request skill review.

## Availability-request freshness and completion

- **2026-08-27 — Availability update timestamp and collaboration (Chris):** Chris needs the date and time of the last availability update visible in the same request detail on both the Nursery Portal and LPS’s view, and asked whether the nursery’s and LPS’s concurrent updates reflect between them or whether LPS is master. Today the nursery signals it has completed an availability request by pressing **Submit**: the request becomes Submitted and receives a submission time; Processing means it has saved at least one line but not completed the handoff. Both views write the same supplier-request line answers (status, available quantity, price and notes), but they are not live-synced: each only re-reads after its own save, there is no conflict warning or master owner, and the last save wins. The original job-request line is a snapshot, so it does not automatically change from supplier answers and does not rewrite the supplier’s ask. The current generic row modification timestamps cannot yet be presented truthfully as a nursery availability-freshness time: a request-level timestamp may not advance for later line edits, and a line timestamp does not distinguish a nursery update from an LPS edit. Chris must settle freshness meaning, overwrite policy and whether completed responses reconcile to the job request; Bhoumik then decides whether durable workflow data is needed. Chris’s core downstream case is a change after the availability was used in a customer quote: the nursery must be able to re-submit while open, LPS must preserve and compare the prior/new supplier response, flag it through both the pre-confirmation Request and confirmed Order, and leave it pending until Chris acknowledges/resolves it. Rosie should receive the structured change and alert Chris, but not decide the outcome. Source: feedback log, **Availability changes after quote** (2026-08-27). ## Job-request lifecycle

- **2026-08-27 — Chris’s LPS Ops lifecycle and history (Nova handoff; Chris clarification):** Chris’s active lifecycle is Active (green), Quote Delivered (blue), Confirmed (purple), Closed Won (teal/dark green), Closed Lost (red). He has superseded the screenshot’s Archived status: Archived “just takes up data.” A bad or duplicate request is deleted; a legitimate request ends Closed Won (becomes an order) or Closed Lost. Chris has specified the future structured Closed Lost reasons — Price, Different Vendor, Unable to Fulfill Request, No Response, or NA — but marked their capture as low-priority follow-on work, not part of the immediate lifecycle/history build. Completed requests should leave active operational work but remain in a Job Request Historical view for trusted reporting, including request count/dollars created in a period and Closed Won count/dollars in a period; the loss reason should remain visible/queryable when that follow-on ships. The exact Confirmed→Closed Won event, migration of existing statuses (including historic lost reasons), retention/deletion safeguards, date bases, and final scope remain unconfirmed. Current system treats only Sourcing and Quoted as open and closes the JR thread at Closed Won, Closed Lost, or Archived.
