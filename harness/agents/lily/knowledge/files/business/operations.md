## Supplier availability outreach

- **2026-08-28 — Non-Partner availability emails should read as a normal human ask (Chris, JR-21 test):** Suppliers do not understand internal LPS terminology, so an availability email must not describe its purpose as an “LPS job.” Chris wants a brief, light, informal request with concrete project/location context where known and appropriate, followed by the plant, size and quantity and a direct question about whether it is available. “Please confirm your availability” is too demanding; asking “Y’all have those available?” is the intended posture. This applies to the Chris-approved, non-Partner outreach flow; recorded supplier-specific preferences still win. The original job-request line is a snapshot, so it does not automatically change from supplier answers and does not rewrite the supplier’s ask. Chris’s core downstream case is a change after the availability was used in a customer quote: the nursery must be able to re-submit while open, LPS must preserve and compare the prior/new supplier response, flag it through both the pre-confirmation Request and confirmed Order, and leave it pending until Chris acknowledges/resolves it. This conflicts with Bhoumik’s 2026-08-28 proposal to close all availability requests at Confirmed/order creation: closing there would block the late supplier correction Chris specifically needs to see during the confirmed-Order period. Chris must decide the terminal point for availability revision. A supplier-facing Order Details link is a future enhancement, not a dependency for dispatch or response handling. Chris has made both the compact customer Job Request Details workspace and initial supplier order-email dispatch near-term utility, rather than future polish; the durable approved-projection/review model and order/reply correlation are now urgent foundations. ## Job-request lifecycle

- **2026-08-27 — Chris’s LPS Ops lifecycle and history (Nova handoff; Chris clarification):** Chris’s active lifecycle is Active (green), Quote Delivered (blue), Confirmed (purple), Closed Won (teal/dark green), Closed Lost (red). He has superseded the screenshot’s Archived status: Archived “just takes up data.” A bad or duplicate request is deleted; a legitimate request ends Closed Won (becomes an order) or Closed Lost. Chris has specified the future structured Closed Lost reasons — Price, Different Vendor, Unable to Fulfill Request, No Response, or NA — but marked their capture as low-priority follow-on work, not part of the immediate lifecycle/history build. Completed requests should leave active operational work but remain in a Job Request Historical view for trusted reporting, including request count/dollars created in a period and Closed Won count/dollars in a period; the loss reason should remain visible/queryable when that follow-on ships. The exact Confirmed→Closed Won event, migration of existing statuses (including historic lost reasons), retention/deletion safeguards, date bases, and final scope remain unconfirmed. Current system treats only Sourcing and Quoted as open and closes the JR thread at Closed Won, Closed Lost, or Archived. Sources: feedback log, **LPS Ops job statuses**, **No archived requests**, and **Closed Lost reason required** (2026-08-27); schema/app/docs review.

- **2026-08-28 — Confirmed, fulfillment, and closure propagation:** Confirmed means availability and quote work/back-and-forth are complete, the customer has been shown what is available and its cost, and has committed to buy; an order may then be created and processed while the request remains Confirmed. Closed Won is only when the plants have been delivered or picked up and the transaction took place — not when an order ID is created. The agreed terminal closure point is Closed Won/Lost, but Chris must still resolve Bhoumik’s proposal to close supplier availability requests earlier at Confirmed/order creation against the need for supplier revision/re-submission during fulfillment. Sources: feedback log, **Bhoumik’s lifecycle proposal**, **Confirmed means committed**, and **Closed Won means fulfilled** (2026-08-28).

- **2026-08-28 — A quote is a cycle, not a one-way stage:** A customer can change a job after the first quote — adding/removing plants or accepting substitutions — so the same Job Request can need multiple quotes. All quote cycles must be historically retained and reportable. Chris chose the sales-pipeline labels: **Active** while LPS works/reworks the request and **Quote Delivered** after the customer receives the latest complete pricing-and-availability package; a customer change returns the request to Active, and revised quote delivery returns it to Quote Delivered. Chris rejected the longer “Quote and Availability Delivered” label; concise Quote Delivered stays, with descriptive/help text making its full meaning clear. Source: feedback log, **Quotes can recur** and **Quote Delivered stays** (2026-08-28).

- **2026-08-28 — Confirmed carries the supplier conversation through fulfillment (Chris):** Supplier changes “occur regularly” after customer confirmation. Confirmed therefore creates/surfaces a supplier **Order Details** inbox item, carrying forward the request information and the same communication/update functionality; supplier coordination continues through fulfilment, rather than closing at order creation. The terminal point remains Closed Won/Lost, when the transaction completes or is lost. Source: feedback log, **Confirmed carries supplier communication** (2026-08-28).

- **2026-08-29 — Quick quote is an opportunity, not sourcing work (Chris):** At volume, contractors’ early budget-price requests are managed in a separate Quote view, outside Request Management. LPS returns the quick quote there; no Job Request, supplier availability ask, or Rosie JR thread begins merely because it exists. When the customer returns to source plants or revise installation timing, quote details create a **new** linked Request and Request Management begins. The original quote remains evidence/context, not current supplier-confirmed price or availability. This separation is needed for truthful work queues and reporting: quote opportunities/conversions are distinct from operational Job Requests and their confirmed/closed outcomes. Source: feedback log, **Quick quotes are not Job Requests** (2026-08-29).
