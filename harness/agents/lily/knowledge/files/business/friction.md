## Unowned supplier-response collaboration

- **2026-08-27 — Availability collaboration and master (Chris):** A nursery and LPS edit the same supplier-request answer fields, but neither open screen receives the other side’s changes automatically; each sees its own post-save re-read and must refresh/reopen to catch the other side. There is no source-of-truth owner, edit history, lock, conflict warning or explicit override: the latest save simply replaces the earlier value. The original job-request line is a separate snapshot, so it neither updates the supplier’s request after it is sent nor receives completed supplier answers automatically. This creates a real sourcing risk: Chris cannot tell whether an answer is the nursery’s current statement or an LPS entry, and may see two related records disagree without a defined reconciliation step. A Last updated timestamp without actor/provenance would obscure rather than solve that risk. This becomes operationally material after a quote: a nursery’s changed availability must be compared against what it previously submitted and remain visible to Chris from both a still-open Request and a confirmed Order until resolved. Source: feedback log, **Availability collaboration and master** and **Availability changes after quote** (2026-08-27); app/schema review.
