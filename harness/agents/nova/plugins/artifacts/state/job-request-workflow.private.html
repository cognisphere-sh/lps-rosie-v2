Current Rosie workflow · 18 September 2026

  1. 1

    Capture · Quote

    Rosie

    Order/quick quote? Create/reuse one Job Request and its lines. Expected install date may be Unknown.

    Price question? Answer only—no Job Request or availability request.

  2. 2

    Price & draft

    Rosie

    Use Austin Plant Supply unless Chris authorizes otherwise. Quote the cheapest priced requested option. Chris approves alternatives separately for quoting and supplier asks. Price is not stock confirmation.

    Chris-approved draft → Customer
    Independent delivery; not automatic.

    2 ⇢ 6 · Direct-order checkSource includes quote → Order without availability; rollout unverified. Rosie’s instructions use answered availability records. Current admin-override workaround needs separate publication approval—not invented supplier confirmation.
  3. 3

    Stop or source

    Chris

    Chris approves supplier publication after quoting; delivery starts Availability.

    Quick quote stops—no availability request or follow-up approval ask. Resume on the same job.

  4. 4

    Answer

    Supplier

    Save keeps edits; Submit/Re-submit sends for review. Availability-request Completed means answered, not ordered. Responses do not automatically change the job or notify customers.

  5. 5

    Choose

    Chris

    Compare suppliers separately. Chris selects the source and authorizes job-line availability, quantities and offering changes. Neither cheapest price nor one reply selects a supplier.

  6. Customer · Independent input
    Accepts exact lines/quantities.
    6

    Commit

    Rosie

    Create separate supplier-visible Orders; job becomes Committed. Allocate shared quantities once across jobs—never duplicate demand.

  7. 7

    Prepare & fulfil

    Supplier + Chris
    Order: New→Processing→Order Ready

    Confirmation records supplier fulfillment date; Chris controls pickup/delivery. Expected install date is separate. Ready is not completed delivery.

  8. 8

    Close

    Chris

    Record Won/Lost explicitly; acceptance and readiness do not close the job. Partial demand may remain open. Reopening restores linked availability work, not a terminal Order.

Not a compulsory staircase

Revise quotes without moving later jobs backward. Shared availability work closes only after every linked job ends.

Notes

Customer: original requests. Shared: customer-useful exceptions. Sourcing: internal decisions. Supplier notes never automatically become Shared.

Approvals: Chris’s original chat; exact authorized scope.
