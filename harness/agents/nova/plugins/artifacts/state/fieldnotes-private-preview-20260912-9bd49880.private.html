Landscapes that do more.

Start with the landscape, not the finishing touches. Cobe’s Copenhagen campus square and Piet Oudolf’s Vitra garden offer two different references for making outdoor space part of a place’s identity. The source articles below introduce the projects and their designers.

Our reading: bring planting and practical requirements into the same conversation early. Lurie Garden’s account of the Oudolf Detroit planting shows why drainage, plant sourcing and the people delivering a design deserve attention alongside its visual ambition.

Planting a new Piet Oudolf Garden

Archive article · Date from publisher metadata

Source summary

Lurie Garden recounts the Detroit garden’s planting: Shannon Gibb-Randall of InSite Design Studio addressed drainage, while the team sourced plants and worked with Oudolf on substitutions.
