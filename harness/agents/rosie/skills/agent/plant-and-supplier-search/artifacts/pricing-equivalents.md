## Likely equivalent names versus actual alternatives

Chris authorized direct pricing for plants that are most likely the same
under different requested/catalogue names (2026-09-11). Check common and
botanical names, aliases, cultivar/form information, requested size and source
context. When that evidence supports a likely naming equivalent, use its
positive, applicable price directly for the requested row. Do not label it an
alternative or ask for substitution approval merely because its catalogue
name differs. Preserve the requested name and necessary source evidence.
Keep a concise internal mapping/basis only when it materially qualifies identity
or pricing, e.g. `Agave Parryi pricing basis; truncata identity unconfirmed`.
Routine exact-name/spelling matches need no note if accessible evidence already
suffices; do not repeat structured size/price. Chris confirmed `Magnolia Southern`
and `Southern Magnolia` should not be treated as different plants merely because
name terms are reversed (JR-79 webchat message 16, 2026-09-24). Search both
orders and verify the full plant identity and size; use the matching price as
name-equivalent evidence, not as a substitution or unverified stock claim.
No invented evidence.

**When a requested botanical/common name has no supported supplier price**
(Chris, JR-78 webchat message 20, 2026-09-24):
1. After searching the requested name and ordinary catalogue variants, research
   other documented common/botanical/trade names on the internet. Record the
   source URL and what relationship it actually supports; a search snippet,
   similar appearance or shared genus alone does not establish synonymy.
2. Re-search the *selected supplier's* price list under each supported candidate
   name at the requested size. Inspect full botanical, common, cultivar/form and
   size identity, positive current price and source context; distinguish a
   defensible naming equivalent from a different cultivar/form or a pricing
   proxy. Keep the customer's requested name unchanged. Report the alternate
   name, source/basis and supplier listing as evidence, or retain an unresolved
   price gap if the relationship or price is unsupported. Neither a web name
   nor a price-list hit proves live stock, customer acceptance, substitution or
   an exact supplier AR mapping. Do not widen beyond APS for job pricing without
   Chris's explicit exception.
3. A pricing match does **not** authorize a persistent `plant_aliases` entry.
   Show Chris the specific proposed requested-name ↔ other-name relationship
   and its identity evidence, and wait for his explicit confirmation that it is
   an alias (not merely an approved price proxy). Only then coordinate with
   Daisy, who owns inventory/alias records, to store the confirmed relationship
   in `plant_aliases` and verify the saved scope. If he confirms only the price
   basis or identity remains uncertain, leave `plant_aliases` unchanged;
   preserve any scoped pricing permission separately under the applicable rule.
